KATMANDU FOR 3 DAYS CULTURAL WONDERS TOUR

  • DESCRIPTION
  • PROGRAM
  • Map

Embark on a 2-night, 3-day Exclusive Pashupatinath Darshan Tour from Gorakhpur to Kathmandu, a journey that blends spirituality, culture, and scenic beauty. Explore Nepal’s iconic temples like Pashupatinath, Guhjeshwari, and Manokamna, and delve into Buddhist heritage at Lumbini, the birthplace of Lord Buddha. Enjoy a comfortable stay, expert guidance, and a serene road trip through lush valleys and majestic landscapes.

This well-structured itinerary offers divine experiences and cultural insight, perfect for every traveler. Discover ancient stupas, bustling markets, and tranquil surroundings while benefiting from hassle-free arrangements, including currency exchange assistance and bilingual guides for a smooth, enriching journey.

Program ( 4 days )

Begin your journey from Gorakhpur, exploring Gorakhnath Temple, Lumbini, and Kathmandu's sacred sites like Pashupatinath and Swayambhunath, before returning with unforgettable spiritual and cultural memories.

  • Day 1

    Spiritual Border Journey

    Begin near AFC Manas Hotel, visit Gorakhnath Temple, explore Lumbini via Sonauli, and enjoy a scenic drive to Kathmandu.

  • Day 2

    Kathmandu Spiritual Exploration

    Begin with breakfast, visit Pashupatinath, Guhjeshwari, Boudhanath, Buddhanilkantha, and Swayambhunath, ending with a peaceful overnight stay in Kathmandu.

  • Day 3

    Return with Memories

    Enjoy the scenic return journey to Gorakhpur, proceed to the station or airport, and conclude with cherished spiritual and cultural memories.
